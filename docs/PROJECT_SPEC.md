# LYKYOU Space — Project Specification v1.0

## Identity
- Name: LYKYOU Space
- Tagline: Your people. Your space. Your LYKN universe.
- Audience: Indian LYKYOUs
- Operator: LYKN India Fanclub
- Status: Independent and unofficial; no implied affiliation with LYKN or GMMTV.

## Member profiles
Display name, unique username, avatar, bio, optional city/state, favourite song/era/moments, fan journey, interests, optional social links. Custom field-level privacy. Bias and bias-wrecker each support multiple choices from WILLIAM, LEGO, NUT, HONG, TUI, including OT5 and blank.

## Registration and access
Open registration. Public can view public feed content; authenticated members can publish and interact. Private messages are limited to accepted friends.

## Social features
Text/photo/link posts, polls, questions, likes, comments/replies, follows, friend requests, friend-only direct messages, group conversations, bookmarks, search, notifications.

## Groups
Bias, city/state, general, fan projects, streaming/comeback. Initial groups are admin-created or approved.

## Events
Calendar, online/offline events, registration/RSVP, reminders, attendance, photo galleries.

## Fan activities
Streaming campaigns, birthdays, collaborative projects, quizzes, challenges, giveaways, missions and achievements.

## LYKN India hub
Announcements, comeback/release updates, schedules/appearances, news/interviews, fanclub campaigns, streaming guides and resources. Distinguish confirmed information from rumours; prefer official sources.

## Safety and moderation
Reports, admin review, profanity/inappropriate-content filters, spam/fake-account controls, block/restrict, community guidelines, suspension and appeals. Role-based access for members, moderators and admins.

## Additional features
Search, in-app notifications, dark/light themes, mobile-first layout, badges, leaderboards, bookmarks, admin dashboard and analytics.

## Proposed later enhancements
- LYKYOU Space Passport: member journey and achievements.
- Memory Galaxy: curated fan memories and milestones.
- Fan Project Wall: project tasks, volunteering and progress.
- Space Countdown: birthdays, releases and fan events, displayed in IST where appropriate.
- Meet the LYKYOUs: opt-in member introductions.
- Space Radio: curated official streaming links/playlists, not unlicensed hosting.
- Fan Milestone Timeline.
- Moderated merchandise information board, without payment processing.

## Privacy and security principles
Never expose email addresses, passwords, private contact details, or secret API keys. Make location/social links optional. Apply row-level security to user-owned data. Validate uploads and limit file types/sizes. Provide block/report controls. Admin powers must be server-enforced, not merely hidden in the UI.
